Layout options

7 directions for the new app layout. Open each on your phone and pick the one that feels right.

OPTION 1

Minimalist — "Just run."

One focus per screen: giant timer, one Start button, everything else tucked behind a single "More" disclosure. Maximum breathing room.

OPTION 2

Dashboard

Options-heavy athletic grid: stat tiles, workout hero, week-plan strip, music card, map snapshot, settings. Everything glanceable.

Map-first

Full-screen route map hero with a floating glass timer card — live km, pace and time over the route. Strava energy.

OPTION 4

Bottom-nav

True native-app feel: 4 thumb-friendly tabs (Today / Plan / Map / Music), one compact screen each, no long scroll.

OPTION 5

Typographic

Brutalist Nike-ad energy: massive condensed type, aggressive motivational microcopy, volt-on-black poster look.

OPTION 6

Card-deck

The workout as swipeable full-screen cards — Warmup, Intervals, Cooldown, Done — with progress dots. One card at a time.

Compact

The entire workout above the fold on a phone screen: timer, controls, up-next, mini stats. Zero scroll.
