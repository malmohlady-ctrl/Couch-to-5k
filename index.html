Start slower than you think you need to. Walking breaks are part of the plan, not a failure.

Wear comfortable shoes, choose a flat route, and finish feeling like you could do a little more.

Before you go

  • Walk easily for the first 5 minutes.
  • Run at a comfortable, conversational effort — not a sprint.
  • Stop if you feel chest pain, faintness, or unusual shortness of breath.

Your 9-week plan

Aim for three non-consecutive days per week, with rest or gentle walking between sessions. If a week feels too hard, repeat it.

How to progress

The goal is consistency, not speed. Keep running intervals easy, take walking breaks as prescribed, and repeat a week whenever you need more time.
