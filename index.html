The full journey

9-week training plan

Aim for three non-consecutive days per week. Workouts are time-based, not distance-based. Repeat any session or week whenever you need more time.

How to make it stick

Pick three repeatable days. Keep the running portions easy enough to speak in short sentences. On rest days, rest or take a gentle walk. Your goal is consistency, not speed.

Install on your phone

Once this app is hosted securely (HTTPS), open it in Chrome on Android, tap the browser menu, then choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”. It will get its own icon and launch like an app. The installation prompt varies by browser.

This web app can cache its interface for offline opening after installation. GPS needs location permission. Browser timers and spoken audio may still be suspended by Android if the page is backgrounded or the phone locks; the keep-awake option reduces this risk but cannot guarantee native-app behaviour.

Privacy

This app has no account, analytics, advertising, paid API, or server-side database. Your progress is saved locally. GPS is only used after you tap Enable GPS and grant browser permission; coordinates are not uploaded by this app.
